# Native AX timeout candidate, 2026-11-12

An experimental build increased the per-subscription AX answer wait from 2 to 3 seconds while retaining the existing overall deadline. The hypothesis was that some slow answers might complete without another subscription. This was tested on one iPhone 15 Pro Max using the native Poki MCP. It is **not adopted**: the small sample is insufficient to establish a reliable speedup and one candidate reset stopped after clearing the field because the cleared state could not be verified. The field was independently observed and restored without repeating an uncertain paste.

Two matched Google Translate fill operations with exact text verification took 20.607 and 36.816 seconds with the old helper, and 17.060 and 17.869 seconds with the candidate. The two operations used distinct fixed phrases and corresponding starting states. One language-swap action with a positive final language check took 42.824 seconds before and 13.675 seconds with the candidate. Times are caller wall time; each is a single observation, not a distribution or evidence of causation. A candidate reset failed with `clear_not_verified`; it was not counted as a successful timed fill. The very high variability and failure prevent a claim that the candidate is faster or safe for all workflows.

The public candidate passed 42 native tests after local network permissions were granted. The initial sandboxed run passed 36 and failed six loopback tests with `Operation not permitted`. The integrated private-overlay candidate had a source/binary receipt and was observed running on the device. After testing, the change was reverted and the prior helper and supervised service restored; the original build identity and the intended translation text were independently observed. K1 remained paused, and its uncertain unsent record was not replayed.

This is not a completed ten-app benchmark or proof of faster general typing. The calendar event and reservation enquiry had already been submitted once before this experiment and were not repeated.
